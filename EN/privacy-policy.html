    More About Cookies

    What is a cookie file?

    A cookie is a small text file created upon a visit to a website. It serves as a standard tool for the storage of information about visits to our website and how our website is used.

    Thus we can distinguish (but not identify) individual users and customise the content to individual preferences. Cookies are important and web browsing would be far more difficult without them.

    What are cookies used for?

    Cookies serve several purposes. We use the following cookies on our e-shop website and in our mobile applications:

    Technical cookies: We use technical cookies to ensure our e-shop works properly (e.g. for you to create a user profile, log in, and buy goods and services). The e-shop would not work without these cookies.

    Functional cookies: These help so that you do not have to log in and set your preferences repeatedly (e.g. setting the language in which our e-shop will be displayed).

    Social network cookies: These cookies allow us to connect you comfortably with your social network profile and for example share products and services with friends or family.

    Personalization of content: these cookies allow us to display content and advertisements based on the data available about you in such a way that we can best tap into your needs. This is particularly about what content you have viewed, or what device you are accessing on our site. It does not concern the personalisation of content on the e-shop www.alza.cz.

    Unpersonalised advertising: these cookies allow us to show you general advertising for products and services.

    Personalized advertising: thanks to these cookies, we and our partners can offer you relevant products and services on a tailored basis based on your purchases, your behavior and your preferences. It does not apply to the personalized e-shop advertisement www.alza.cz.

    Audience measurement: These cookies allow us to optimise our website for your comfort of use, based on how you use it (e.g. functions used, their placement, behaviour on the site). The aim is to remember or anticipate your choices.

    Third-party cookies: These cookies are provided by third parties. You can learn more about them and about our partners here.

    More about SDKs

    More about SDKs

    An SDK (Software Development Kit) is a set of tools, libraries and code that developers integrate into a mobile application to enable it to use certain functions or services. Unlike cookies, which operate in web browsers, SDKs work directly within the mobile application.

    SDKs allow us, for example, to measure how our application is used, to obtain technical information about the device (e.g. phone model, operating system version), to improve stability and to add new features. We use the data to analyse and improve the user experience.

    What do we use SDKs for?

    In our mobile applications, we use various types of SDKs for the following purposes:

    Essential (cannot be disabled)

    • These technologies are necessary for the basic operation and secure functioning of the application.

    Functional

    • Technologies that ensure the smooth operation of certain application features.

    Analytical

    • These technologies help us analyse user behaviour in order to improve the application.

    Marketing

    • Technologies used to display relevant advertising and measure its effectiveness.

    How can you control which SDKs we use?

    You can control the use of certain SDKs (in particular advertising and analytical ones) directly in the application settings or at the level of your device settings (e.g. limiting ad tracking on iOS or Android). However, disabling certain SDKs may limit some application features or its personalisation, which are not essential to the functioning of the application.
