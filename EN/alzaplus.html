FAQ

What is AlzaPlus+ and when can I get free delivery?

AlzaPlus+ is a paid customer program whose members get free shipping for their purchases to AlzaBoxes, branch stores, and and pick up points (Packeta, PPL Parcel Shop, Czech Post, AlzaDrive) along with special discounts. There is no limit to the number of purchases within the membership, so you can shop without delivery fees every day if you like.

Free delivery applies to the entire product range, i.e. all products that can be delivered to a store, AlzaBox, or other pick up points (delivery options are shown in the shopping cart). In exceptional cases, it may happen that we are unable to deliver a particular item using any of the methods listed above. In such cases, free delivery does not apply to that item.

Free delivery includes only selected delivery methods and does not apply to additional paid services (such as cash on delivery).

How can I get an AlzaPlus+ membership, and how do I activate it?

You can get AlzaPlus+ by clicking here nor by activating it in your cart when selecting delivery options. If you purchase the membership while buying another product, the free delivery applies to that purchase as well. Find out how to activate your membership in our guide.

AlzaPlus+ membership can also be obtained as a gift with selected products. Please note that if you purchase multiple products, the gift is only provided once. The membership is activated automatically.

How much does AlzaPlus+ cost and how long do I pay for it?

AlzaPlus+ costs 25 CZK per month (when paid annually at 299 CZK per year). The membership is valid for 365 days from the date of purchase.

If you'd just like to try AlzaPlus+ or purchase it for a shorter period, you can choose a monthly membership for 59 CZK. The monthly option is valid for 30 days from the date of purchase.

If the membership is paid for by card, it renews automatically.

Can I try the program for free?

We do not offer a free trial period, but you can activate the program for a month at the cost of 59 CZK. If you make more than one purchase in a month, the free delivery benefits will have already paid off the membership fee.

You can also receive an AlzaPlus+ membership as a gift when purchasing selected products. Please note that if you purchase multiple products, the gift is only provided once. The membership is activated automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your membership within 14 days of purchase or automatic renewal. In that case, we will cancel your membership immediately and refund your membership fee, less the standard value of any benefits you have already used. If you have not used the membership at all, you may withdraw from it at any time.

If the membership was paid for by card, it renews automatically. You can also switch off automatic renewal at any time directly on the Alza.cz website after signing in to your account. Your membership will then no longer renew automatically and will end once the paid membership period expires. For example, if a monthly membership was purchased on 1 April at 15:00 and cancelled on 20 April, you can still enjoy free delivery until 30 April at 15:00. After that, standard delivery charges will apply.

Can I use AlzaPlus+ as an entrepreneur?

Yes, you can also enjoy the benefits of AlzaPlus+ as an entrepreneur.

What is the difference between AlzaPlus+ and Alza Premium?

Alza Premium still exists. Compared to AlzaPlus+, it offers more benefits and delivery to your address. You can find more details about  Alza Premium here.

Where can I find AlzaPlus+ member discounts?

We announce discounts on our website and, where appropriate, through other communication channels such as e-mail and app push notifications. The discounts are offered occasionally, and we want them to be worthwhile.

How do I redeem a voucher from a friend?

Open www.alza.cz/alzaplus-referral. Enter your friend's code in the "Activation code" field and click "Activate". Then simply complete your order and start enjoying discounts and free delivery with AlzaPlus+.
