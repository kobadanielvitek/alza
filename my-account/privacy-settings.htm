    Cookies necessary for the website to function properly

    These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and they cannot be deactivated. In addition, these files contribute to the safe and proper use of our services.Cannot be disabled.

    Functional cookies

    These cookies enable us to remember your basic choices and improve your user experience.

    Analytical cookies

    Analytical cookies on our website are collected by a script by Google Inc., which then anonymises this data. After anonymisation, they are no longer personal data, because anonymised cookies cannot be assigned to a specific user, resp., specific person. We only work with cookies in an anonymised form. Therefore, we cannot find out from cookies how a particular user behaved on our website (what pages he or she visited, what goods he or she viewed, etc.)

    Audience measurement

    These cookies enable us to optimise our site for your convenience based on how you use it. The aim is to remember or anticipate your choices. This includes, for example, the use of features, their location and behaviour on the page.
